# School hierarchy

| Korean term | English | Português | Use |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Haksaeng (학생) | Student | Aluno(a) | A person studying in the dojang, at any gub or Dan rank. |
| Yudan-ja (유단자) | Dan holder | Faixa preta graduado(a) | A practitioner who holds a Dan grade. |
| Kyosanim (교사님) | Instructor / teacher | Instrutor(a) | A respectful title for an instructor; usage differs by school. |
| Sabom / Sabomnim (사범 / 사범님) | Instructor / master instructor | Professor(a) / instrutor(a) | A common title for a qualified instructor; exact rank conventions vary. |
| Kwanjangnim (관장님) | School director | Diretor(a) da escola | A respectful title for the person leading a school or dojang. |
| Suseok / Daesabom (수석 / 대사범) | Senior / grandmaster instructor | Instrutor(a) sênior / grão-mestre | Titles used by some organizations for senior teachers; not universal rank labels. |

Titles are conventions, not a universal promotion ladder. A school may use different titles or require specific credentials. Check local usage and address instructors respectfully.

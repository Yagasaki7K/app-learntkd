# Everyday dojang terminology

## Commands

| Romanji | Hangul | English | Português | Explanation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Charyeot | 차렷 | Attention | Atenção | Stand at attention. |
| Gyeongnye | 경례 | Bow | Cumprimentar / reverência | Bow respectfully. |
| Junbi | 준비 | Ready | Preparar | Take the ready position. |
| Sijak | 시작 | Begin | Começar | Begin the exercise. |
| Geuman | 그만 | Stop | Parar | Stop the exercise. |
| Baro | 바로 | Return | Voltar à posição | Return to the starting position. |

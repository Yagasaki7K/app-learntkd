# Kicks

## Ap Chagi
- **Romanji:** Ap Chagi
- **Hangul:** 앞차기
- **English:** Front Kick
- **Portuguese:** Chute frontal
- **Belt:** Branca
- **Belt slug:** 10-gub-white
- **Level:** beginner
- **Category:** kick
- **Purpose:** Ataque frontal direto com a perna da frente ou de trás.
- **Purpose English:** A direct front attack using the lead or rear leg.
- **Execution:** Eleve o joelho, estenda a perna em direção ao alvo com a bola do pé e recolha antes de apoiar.
- **Execution English:** Lift the knee, extend toward the target with the ball of the foot, then recoil before setting the foot down.
- **Pronunciation:** ap tcha-gui

## Dollyo Chagi
- **Romanji:** Dollyo Chagi
- **Hangul:** 돌려차기
- **English:** Roundhouse Kick
- **Portuguese:** Chute circular
- **Belt:** Amarela
- **Belt slug:** 8-gub-yellow
- **Level:** beginner
- **Category:** kick
- **Purpose:** Chute circular para atingir o tronco ou a cabeça com velocidade e rotação do quadril.
- **Purpose English:** A round kick to the trunk or head, driven by speed and hip rotation.
- **Execution:** Gire o pé de apoio, eleve o joelho lateralmente, rotacione o quadril e golpeie com o peito do pé. Recolha a perna com controle.
- **Execution English:** Pivot the support foot, chamber the knee to the side, rotate the hip and strike with the instep. Recoil with control.
- **Pronunciation:** dol-lyo tcha-gui

## Yeop Chagi
- **Romanji:** Yeop Chagi
- **Hangul:** 옆차기
- **English:** Side Kick
- **Portuguese:** Chute lateral
- **Belt:** Verde
- **Belt slug:** 6-gub-green
- **Level:** beginner
- **Category:** kick
- **Purpose:** Chute de empurrão com alcance e alinhamento lateral.
- **Purpose English:** A pushing kick with reach and lateral alignment.
- **Execution:** Vire o corpo de lado, traga o joelho ao peito e empurre o calcanhar em linha reta. Recolha antes de retornar à base.
- **Execution English:** Turn the body sideways, chamber the knee, and drive the heel straight out. Recoil before returning to stance.
- **Pronunciation:** yop tcha-gui

## Dwi Chagi
- **Romanji:** Dwi Chagi
- **Hangul:** 뒤차기
- **English:** Back Kick
- **Portuguese:** Chute para trás
- **Belt:** Azul
- **Belt slug:** 4-gub-blue
- **Level:** intermediate
- **Category:** kick
- **Purpose:** Contra-ataque direto para trás, aproveitando o giro e a distância.
- **Purpose English:** A direct counter kick to the rear that uses rotation and distance.
- **Execution:** Olhe por cima do ombro, alinhe o quadril e empurre o calcanhar para trás sem abrir o tronco.
- **Execution English:** Look over the shoulder, align the hip and drive the heel backward without opening the torso.
- **Pronunciation:** dui tcha-gui

## Naeryo Chagi
- **Romanji:** Naeryo Chagi
- **Hangul:** 내려차기
- **English:** Axe Kick
- **Portuguese:** Chute descendente
- **Belt:** Vermelha
- **Belt slug:** 2-gub-red
- **Level:** intermediate
- **Category:** kick
- **Purpose:** Chute descendente que combina elevação, controle e queda do calcanhar.
- **Purpose English:** A descending kick that combines elevation, control and a heel drop.
- **Execution:** Eleve a perna estendida pela frente ou lateral, mantenha o tronco estável e desça o calcanhar com controle.
- **Execution English:** Raise the extended leg to the front or side, keep the torso stable and lower the heel with control.
- **Pronunciation:** ne-ryo tcha-gui

## Dwi Huryeo Chagi
- **Romanji:** Dwi Huryeo Chagi
- **Hangul:** 뒤후려차기
- **English:** Spinning Hook Kick
- **Portuguese:** Chute gancho giratório
- **Belt:** Vermelha ponta preta
- **Belt slug:** 1-gub-red-black-tip
- **Level:** intermediate
- **Category:** kick
- **Purpose:** Técnica de giro para atingir lateralmente com o calcanhar ou a sola.
- **Purpose English:** A spinning technique that strikes laterally with the heel or sole.
- **Execution:** Gire olhando primeiro para o alvo, mantenha o eixo e descreva um arco com a perna estendida; recolha para recuperar a base.
- **Execution English:** Turn the head to find the target first, stay on axis and arc the extended leg; recoil to regain stance.
- **Pronunciation:** dui hu-ryo tcha-gui

## Twio Ap Chagi
- **Romanji:** Twio Ap Chagi
- **Hangul:** 뛰어앞차기
- **English:** Jumping Front Kick
- **Portuguese:** Chute frontal saltando
- **Belt:** Preta (Dan)
- **Belt slug:** black-dan
- **Level:** advanced
- **Category:** kick
- **Purpose:** Aplicação dinâmica do chute frontal com deslocamento e aterrissagem equilibrada.
- **Purpose English:** A dynamic front kick with forward travel and a balanced landing.
- **Execution:** Carregue a base, salte na direção do alvo, execute Ap Chagi e aterrisse com os joelhos flexionados e guarda recomposta.
- **Execution English:** Load the stance, jump toward the target, perform Ap Chagi and land with knees flexed and guard restored.
- **Pronunciation:** ttwi-o ap tcha-gui
